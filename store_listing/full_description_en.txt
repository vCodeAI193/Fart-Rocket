Fart Rocket is a physics-based 2D arcade game: pilot a stick figure that
launches itself through obstacle levels with fart-powered bursts. Drag to
aim, release to thrust — master saws, lasers, spinning wheels, and bosses,
collect coins and stars, unlock cosmetics, and try game modes like Time
Attack, Hardcore, Zen, or Ghost Race. Fully procedurally rendered, no
external art assets.
